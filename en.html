The life of a stand: eight stages

01 / 08

Design

It starts with the brief.

We take the project brief and work out, together with you, what your brand needs at the event. The 3D design is planned not only for how it looks, but also for modularity, transport and repeated installation.

  • Brief analysis
  • 3D design
  • Technical drawings

02 / 08

Production

The approved design goes into production.

The stand is built in our workshop, and technical preparation is completed at this stage. Before anything leaves for the venue, the parts are pre-assembled and the lighting and screens are tested.

  • Workshop production
  • Technical preparation
  • Pre-assembly and lighting test

03 / 08

Storage

Parts are packed and stored.

The stand is taken apart, packed in boxes and labelled. It is kept in our warehouse, with an inventory record, until the next event.

  • Packing
  • Labelling
  • Inventory tracking

04 / 08

Transport

Planned shipping, on-time delivery.

The packed stand is shipped on schedule to the city hosting the event. Because ease of transport is considered at the design stage, logistics stay predictable.

  • Planned shipping
  • Intercity logistics
  • On-time delivery

05 / 08

Installation

The stand is installed at the venue.

Our site team installs the stand at the venue; the platform, counter, screens and lighting are commissioned. The stand is checked and handed over before the opening.

  • On-site coordination
  • Assembly
  • Lighting and screen commissioning

06 / 08

Dismantling

Controlled dismantling after the event.

When the event ends, the stand is dismantled in a controlled way. The parts are counted, packed and sent back to the warehouse.

  • Controlled dismantling
  • Parts count
  • Packing

07 / 08

Maintenance

Every part is checked.

Each part returning to the warehouse is checked one by one, and any repair or refurbishment is carried out. The stand is made ready for the next event.

  • Parts inspection
  • Repair and refurbishment
  • Quality approval

08 / 08

Reuse

The same stand, different events.

A stand is rarely limited to a single event. The same parts are installed again throughout the year, in different cities, in different floor sizes and for changing needs.

  • Different city
  • Different size
  • Same brand language

Approach

How long it lasts matters as much as how it looks.

A stand is rarely used for just one event. The same stand may need to be installed again throughout the year, in different cities, in different floor sizes and for changing needs. We design with that in mind.

  1. i.

    Modularity

    Parts are designed to fit different floor sizes; the same stand is rearranged and used in different layouts.

  2. ii.

    Reusability

    The stand is designed not for a single event, but to be installed again in different cities throughout the year.

  3. iii.

    Easy to transport and store

    Every part is planned to be easy to carry, pack and store; logistics is part of the design.

  4. iv.

    A long-term partner

    Instead of looking for a new supplier for every event, you work with a team that knows your brand and your needs.

About

25+ years · congress and exhibition stands

More than 25 years of experience in stand design.

Nilus is a design and build company specialising in the design, production and installation of congress and exhibition stands.

For more than 25 years we have worked with brands in the pharmaceutical and healthcare sector, and in the coffee sector as well. We treat a brand's needs at congresses and exhibitions not just as stand design, but as a process to be managed from start to finish.

The process begins with taking the project brief and preparing the 3D design, and continues with production, technical preparation, transport and installation at the venue. After the event, we also dismantle, store and maintain the stand and prepare it for the next one.
